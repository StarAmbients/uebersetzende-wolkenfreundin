# 🌟 Code of Conduct
### Übersetzende Wolkenfreundin – Community & Contribution Guidelines

This project exists to support translators, collaborators, and the broader Bruno Gröning Circle of Friends with clarity, kindness, and technical excellence.
We aim to create a collaborative environment that reflects respect, goodwill, and a shared sense of service.

This Code of Conduct applies to all contributors, maintainers, users, and participants interacting with the project.

---

## 💛 Our Values

We strive to build a community where:

- Kindness guides communication
- Everyone feels welcome and respected
- Collaboration uplifts all participants
- Mistakes are treated as learning opportunities
- Technical work is performed with integrity and care
- Spiritual values of harmony, clarity, and goodwill are honored

---

## 🤝 Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with **respect, patience, and empathy**
- Assume good intentions in discussions
- Communicate clearly, constructively, and kindly
- Offer help when someone is confused or struggling
- Welcome questions — no question is “too basic”
- Provide thoughtful and compassionate feedback
- Collaborate in a spirit of service and humility
- Use inclusive and positive language

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following is not allowed:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or exclusion of any kind
- Dismissive or hostile communication
- Sharing private or confidential information
- Posting harmful, misleading, or inappropriate content
- Disrespecting spiritual principles or the mission of the project
- Intentionally breaking development workflows
- Abuse of project resources or access privileges

If you’re unsure whether something is appropriate, ask — or choose the more considerate option.

---

## 🧘 Conflict Resolution

When disagreements arise:

1. Pause and breathe
2. Seek clarity — misunderstandings happen
3. Communicate respectfully and directly
4. Involve maintainers if needed

Maintainers will:

- Listen with care
- Seek mutual understanding
- Encourage constructive solutions
- Act in alignment with project values

---

## 🔒 Security and Privacy

Participants must:

- Never share API keys, credentials, `.env` files, or private reports
- Handle sensitive data carefully and discreetly
- Report security issues privately to maintainers

---

## 🛠 Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers commit to:

- Upholding this Code of Conduct
- Reviewing contributions with fairness and kindness
- Creating a safe and supportive collaboration space
- Acting promptly when issues arise
- Leading by example

Maintainers may remove or block contributors who violate this Code of Conduct.
